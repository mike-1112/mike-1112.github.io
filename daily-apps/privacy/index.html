Daily Apps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy applies to the Android apps Scripture Daily, Psalms Daily, Stoic Daily, Torah Daily and Quran Daily, published by Ahmed Zafar Iqbal under the Google Play developer name aazi.

Reading and device features

You can read without creating an app account. The apps do not ask for your name, email, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Reading content is included in the app. App preferences and purchase status may be stored on your device. Clearing app data removes local settings. Notifications are optional and scheduled on your device. Sharing sends the text you choose to the app or service you select, whose own privacy policy then applies. Fonts may load from Google Fonts, which receives ordinary connection information such as an IP address.

Advertising

Free versions use Google AdMob. Depending on app version, device settings and consent, Google's advertising SDK processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics. Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement, reliability and fraud prevention. New releases use general-audience, non-personalized ads and child/under-age treatment; older installed versions may have different settings. Where required, Google's consent interface offers advertising choices. A Privacy choices control is available in supported versions when Google requires it. An active Remove Ads entitlement stops ad requests in the updated app.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising SDK disclosure.

Purchases

Google Play handles payment. The developer does not receive payment card numbers. RevenueCat processes purchase history, transaction receipts, an app-generated customer identifier and entitlement status to validate and restore purchases and provide purchase analytics. Purchase records are retained to support the entitlement, restoration, fraud prevention and applicable recordkeeping requirements. These services encrypt data in transit.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Removing the app does not cancel or erase Google Play purchase records. Use Restore Purchases with the Google Play account used to purchase.

Children and families

The apps do not ask children to create accounts or provide personal contact information. Updated ad requests use child-directed and under-age settings for all users. A parent or guardian should manage purchases through Google Play's parental controls. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information to us.

Support, retention and your choices

If you email support, we receive the address and information you send and use it to respond. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request. Do not send passwords or payment card details. Advertising and purchase providers may process data outside your country under their own privacy policies. You can manage notifications and advertising identifiers in Android settings.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of data associated with these apps, email lyaraconnections@gmail.com with the app name and enough information to identify the relevant support or purchase record. We may need to verify your request. Some transaction information must remain with Google or be retained for legal or fraud-prevention reasons. Deleting purchase entitlement records may affect restoration.
